IMU gimbal rig

Pan / tilt / roll rig with a cam-driven tap striker for testing Adafruit IMU, accelerometer and magnetometer breakouts.

  • Servo console: Web Serial control of the 8 PCA9685 servo channels (Chrome or Edge, board running examples/circuitpython_code.py).
  • Repository and README: CAD generators, STLs, assembly order, fixings.
  • Motion video · Assembly animation
